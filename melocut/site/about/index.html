About MeloCut

MeloCut is a set of free audio tools: a ringtone maker, an audio cutter and joiner, a video to MP3 converter, an audio converter, an AI noise remover and an effects panel. They all run in your web browser.

We made it because most converter sites are slow, covered in pop-ups and want you to upload your personal files. MeloCut runs the FFmpeg audio engine on your own device instead, so it's quicker and your files stay private.
