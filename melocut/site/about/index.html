About MeloCut

MeloCut is a set of free, simple audio tools — a ringtone maker, an audio cutter, a video to MP3 converter and an audio converter — that work entirely in your web browser.

We built it because most converter sites are slow, full of pop-ups and make you upload your personal files. MeloCut runs the FFmpeg audio engine on your own device instead, so it is faster and your files stay private.
