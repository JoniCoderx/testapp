About MeloCut

MeloCut is a set of free audio tools that run in your web browser: a ringtone maker, an audio cutter and joiner, converters for video and audio, an AI noise remover, and effects like slowed + reverb, nightcore, 8D audio and bass boost. There are 18 tools in total, and every one of them works on phones as well as computers.

Why we built it

Most "online MP3 cutter" sites work the same way: you upload your file to their server, wait in a queue, then dodge fake download buttons and pop-ups to get it back. That's slow, and it means a stranger's server gets a copy of your voice notes, recordings and music.

MeloCut does the work on your own device instead. Nothing is uploaded, so it's quicker, it works with large files and bad connections, and your files stay private.

How it works

Under the hood, MeloCut runs FFmpeg, the open source audio and video engine used by VLC, YouTube and countless editing apps. It's compiled to WebAssembly, a format that lets your browser run it at close to native speed. The waveform, playback and equalizer use the browser's built-in Web Audio.

The AI noise remover uses RNNoise, a small open source neural network made by Xiph.org and Mozilla researchers to separate speech from background noise. It also runs on your device.

How the site stays free

MeloCut shows a few ads from Google AdSense, always labelled. There are no paid plans, no sign-up, no limits on how often you use it and no watermark on your audio. See the privacy policy for what that means for your data.

What we care about

  1. Your files are yours. They never leave your device.
  2. Honest buttons. One Download button, no tricks.
  3. Plain answers. Each tool says what it can and can't do, like the vocal remover, which is not AI stem separation.

Contact

Ideas for new tools, bug reports or business questions: hello@melocut.com. If something doesn't work, tell us the file type and your phone or browser, and we'll look into it.
