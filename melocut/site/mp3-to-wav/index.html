When you need a WAV

Some editing programs, samplers and CD burning apps only take WAV. Converting gives you a file they all accept.

One thing to know: converting an MP3 to WAV doesn't bring back quality the MP3 already lost. The WAV sounds the same as the MP3, it's just bigger and easier to edit.

Questions and answers

Will MP3 to WAV improve the quality?

No. It keeps the quality the MP3 has, in a format that is easier to edit.

Why is the WAV so much bigger?

WAV is uncompressed, so it uses about 10 MB per minute.
