How to set your ringtone

On iPhone

Download the M4R file. On a Mac or PC, connect your iPhone and drag the file onto your iPhone in Finder (Mac) or iTunes (Windows). It will appear in Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone. Without a computer, open the file in GarageBand on your iPhone and use Share → Ringtone.

On Android

Download the MP3 file, then go to Settings → Sound → Phone ringtone and choose it (on some phones, tap "+" or "Add ringtone"). You can also move it into the Ringtones folder with the Files app.

Tips for a great ringtone

Start just before the chorus or the most recognisable part, keep it between 20 and 30 seconds, and leave fade in and fade out switched on so it doesn't start or stop abruptly.

Questions & answers

How long can a ringtone be?

iPhone ringtones can be up to 40 seconds. MeloCut limits your selection to 40 seconds so it always works.

What is an M4R file?

M4R is the ringtone format used by iPhone. It is the same as an M4A (AAC) audio file with a different name.

Can I make a ringtone from a video?

Yes. Open any video and MeloCut will use its sound.
