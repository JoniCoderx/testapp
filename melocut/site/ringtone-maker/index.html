Ringtone maker for iPhone and Android

Pick your favorite part of a song and save it as a ringtone for iPhone (M4R) or Android (MP3).

iPhone M4RAndroid MP3Fade in and out

How it works

Choose a song

Open any song or video from your device.

Pick the part you like

Drag on the waveform to select up to 40 seconds. Press Play to check it.

Download the ringtone

Choose M4R for iPhone or MP3 for Android, then Convert and Download.

Setting the ringtone on your phone

iPhone

With a computer: download the M4R file, connect your iPhone and drag the file onto it in Finder (Mac) or iTunes (Windows). It then shows up in Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone. Without a computer, open the file in GarageBand on your iPhone and use Share → Ringtone.

Android

Download the MP3, go to Settings → Sound → Phone ringtone and pick it. On some phones you tap "+" or "Add ringtone" first.

The ringtone guide walks through every step, including the exact menu names on each phone.

A few tips

Start just before the chorus, keep it between 20 and 30 seconds, and leave the fades on so it doesn't start or stop suddenly.

Questions and answers

How long can a ringtone be?

iPhone allows up to 40 seconds, so MeloCut stops your selection there.

What is an M4R file?

It is the ringtone format iPhones use. Inside it is a normal M4A (AAC) audio file.

Can I make a ringtone from a video?

Yes. Open the video and MeloCut uses its sound.
