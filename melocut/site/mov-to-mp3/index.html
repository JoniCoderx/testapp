Why iPhone videos are MOV files

The Camera app saves video in Apple's QuickTime format, which ends in .mov. Inside is normal video and AAC sound, so MeloCut can pull the audio out and save it as an MP3 that plays on any phone, car or computer.

Good uses

Save a song from a concert you filmed, keep the audio of a lecture or a toast, or turn a funny moment into a ringtone. If the recording is noisy, switch on AI noise removal in Effects before you convert.

Big videos

A long 4K video can be several gigabytes. MeloCut handles files up to 1 GB. For longer ones, trim the video in Photos first, or record the part you need again.

Questions and answers

How do I convert a MOV to MP3 on iPhone?

Open this page in Safari, tap the box, choose the video from Photos, then press Convert and Download. No app is needed.

Is the video uploaded?

No. The conversion runs on your phone or computer, so the video never leaves it.

Can I keep the audio as M4A instead?

Yes. Choose M4A in the Format menu. It is the same AAC sound the video already has.
