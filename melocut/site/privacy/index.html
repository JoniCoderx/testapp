Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Your files

MeloCut processes audio and video files entirely inside your web browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers or anyone else's. We can't see, store or share them.

Technical data

Like any website, our hosting provider receives standard technical information when you load a page, such as your IP address and browser type. The audio engine, and the small RNNoise model if you use AI noise removal, are downloaded from the jsDelivr network and then run on your device.

Advertising and cookies

MeloCut may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. More details are in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Analytics

We may use cookie-free analytics to count page views and see which tools are used. It doesn't collect personal data.
