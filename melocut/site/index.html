How to install

How it works

Most converter sites make you upload your file, wait, and download it again. MeloCut runs FFmpeg, the audio engine used by a lot of desktop software, directly in your browser. Your songs, voice notes and videos stay on your device the whole time. There are no pop-ups and no fake download buttons. The site is free because it shows a few ads, and they're always labelled.

Questions and answers

Is MeloCut free?

Yes. Every tool is free, paid for by a few labelled ads. There is no sign-up and no watermark.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens in your browser on your own device.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It works in Safari, Chrome, Firefox and Edge on phones, tablets and computers.

Which file types can I open?

MP3, MP4, MOV, M4A, WAV, OGG, OPUS, FLAC, WebM, AAC, AMR, 3GP and most other audio and video formats.

Can I install MeloCut as an app?

Yes. Add it to your home screen from Safari or Chrome. It opens full screen, and tools you have used before keep working offline. The install guide shows the steps.

Does MeloCut use AI?

The noise remover uses RNNoise, a small neural network that separates speech from background noise. It runs on your device. The other effects are standard audio processing.
