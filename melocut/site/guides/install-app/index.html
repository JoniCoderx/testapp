📲 Install

Install MeloCut on your phone

No app store needed. Add MeloCut to your home screen and it opens full screen like a normal app, and even works offline.

Your browser can install MeloCut right now.

📲 Install app

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open melocut.higgsfield.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open MeloCut from your home screen. It now runs full screen like an app.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open melocut.higgsfield.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app if it pops up, or tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install.
  4. MeloCut appears in your app drawer and on your home screen.

Windows, Mac & Chromebook

  1. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon (a screen with a down arrow) at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Click Install. MeloCut opens in its own window and gets a desktop icon.

Works offline

After you have used a tool once, MeloCut keeps the audio engine on your device, so the tools keep working on a plane or with a bad connection.

Questions & answers

Is the MeloCut app free?

Yes, it is the same free website, just installed on your home screen.

Does it take a lot of space?

No. About 30 MB for the audio engine, much smaller than most apps.

How do I remove it?

Delete it like any other app: long-press the icon and choose Remove.
