Install MeloCut on your phone

You don't need an app store. Add MeloCut to your home screen and it opens full screen like any other app.

Your browser can install MeloCut right now.

Install app

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open melocut.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open MeloCut from your home screen. It now opens full screen like an app.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open melocut.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app if it pops up, or tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (on some phones Add to Home screen), then Install.
  4. MeloCut now shows up in your app drawer and on your home screen.

Windows, Mac and Chromebook

  1. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Click Install. MeloCut opens in its own window and gets a desktop icon.

Using it offline

The first time you use a tool you need an internet connection, because the audio engine (about 30 MB) is downloaded then. After that it is saved on your device, so the tools keep working on a plane or with a bad connection. Open one tool while you're online before you travel.

Questions and answers

Is the app free?

Yes. It is the same free website, just on your home screen.

How much space does it take?

About 30 MB for the audio engine, which is less than most apps.

How do I remove it?

Like any other app: long-press the icon and choose Remove.
