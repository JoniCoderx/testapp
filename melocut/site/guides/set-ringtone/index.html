How to set a custom ringtone

First make your ringtone with the free Ringtone Maker, then follow the steps for your phone.

🔔 Don't have a ringtone yet? Make one in 30 seconds.

Open Ringtone Maker

iPhone without a computer (GarageBand)

  1. In the Ringtone Maker choose MP3 (or M4A) and download your ringtone. It is saved in the Files app, in Downloads.
  2. Install GarageBand (free, from Apple) and open it. Start any instrument, e.g. Audio Recorder.
  3. Tap the tracks view button, then the loop icon (top right) → Files → Browse items from the Files app, and pick your ringtone.
  4. Drag it onto the track, tap the ⌄ arrow (top left) → My Songs.
  5. Long-press the song → Share → Ringtone → Export → Use sound as… Standard Ringtone.

iPhone with a Mac or PC

  1. Choose M4R (iPhone ringtone) in the Ringtone Maker and download it on your computer.
  2. Connect your iPhone with a cable. Open Finder (Mac) or iTunes/Apple Devices (Windows) and select your iPhone.
  3. Drag the .m4r file onto your iPhone in the sidebar. It syncs in seconds.
  4. On the iPhone open Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone and pick it.

Android

  1. Choose MP3 in the Ringtone Maker and download it.
  2. Open Settings → Sound & vibration → Phone ringtone.
  3. Tap +, Add ringtone or My sounds (the name varies by phone) and choose your file from Downloads.
  4. To set it for one contact only: open the contact → ⋮ → Set ringtone.

Tips

Keep ringtones between 20 and 30 seconds, start just before the chorus, and leave the fade in/out on. Raise Volume in Effects if your ringtone is too quiet.

Questions & answers

Why doesn’t my ringtone show up on iPhone?

It must be shorter than 40 seconds and in M4R format when using a computer. MeloCut handles both for you.

Can I set a notification sound too?

Yes. On iPhone choose Text Tone in Sounds & Haptics; on Android choose Notification sound.
