Where are my downloads?

Downloaded a file and can't find it? Here's where it goes on each device.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the Files app → Browse → On My iPhone (or iCloud Drive) → Downloads.
  2. Tap the file to play it, or long-press → Share to send it to WhatsApp, GarageBand or another app.

Android

  1. Open the Files or My Files app → Downloads.
  2. Most music players find new MP3s on their own. If yours doesn't, move the file to the Music folder.

Windows and Mac

  1. Open your Downloads folder in File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac).
  2. In Chrome you can also press Ctrl+J on Windows or ⌘+Shift+J on Mac to see recent downloads.
