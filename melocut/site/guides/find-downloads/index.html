Where are my downloads?

Downloaded a file but can’t find it? Here is where it goes on each device.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the Files app → Browse → On My iPhone (or iCloud Drive) → Downloads.
  2. Tap the file to play it, or long-press → Share to send it to WhatsApp, GarageBand or another app.

Android

  1. Open the Files (or My Files) app → Downloads.
  2. Most music players find new MP3s automatically. If not, move the file to the Music folder.

Windows & Mac

  1. Open your Downloads folder (File Explorer on Windows, Finder on Mac).
  2. In Chrome you can also press Ctrl+J (Windows) or ⌘+Shift+J (Mac) to see recent downloads.
