How to make a ringtone from a video

Got a video with a sound you love, like your kid laughing, a concert clip or a funny moment? Here's how to make it your ringtone.

1. Open the video in the Ringtone Maker

Go to the Ringtone Maker and choose the video from your Photos, Gallery or Files. MeloCut uses the sound and ignores the picture.

2. Pick the part you want

Drag on the waveform to select up to 40 seconds. The loud parts show up as tall bars, which makes the good moments easy to find. Press Play to check.

3. Make it sound good as a ringtone

  1. Leave Fade in and Fade out on so it doesn't start or stop abruptly.
  2. Phone videos are often quiet. Open Effects and turn on Auto loudness.
  3. If there's wind or crowd noise behind a voice, turn on AI noise removal.

4. Save it in the right format

Choose M4R for iPhone or MP3 for Android, then Convert and Download.

5. Set it on your phone

Follow the ringtone setup guide for iPhone (with GarageBand or a computer) or Android.

Questions and answers

Can I use a video I downloaded from social media?

Only use videos you made yourself or have permission to use, and respect copyright.

Why is my ringtone too quiet?

Turn on Auto loudness or raise Volume in Effects before converting.
