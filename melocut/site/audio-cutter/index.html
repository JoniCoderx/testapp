How it works

Open your audio

Choose an MP3, WAV, M4A or any audio or video file.

Select the part

Drag the handles on the waveform, or type exact start and end times.

Keep or remove

Choose Keep selection or Remove selection, then Convert and Download.

Cut audio precisely

Use the waveform to see exactly where the music gets loud or quiet, drag the handles to the right spot and press Play to listen before you save. For millisecond precision, type the start and end time directly (for example 1:23.5).

Remove a part from the middle

Switch to Remove selection to cut out an intro, a silence, a mistake or an ad from a recording. The two remaining parts are joined automatically.

Lossless options

Choose WAV or FLAC to keep full quality, for example when you will edit the file again later.

Questions & answers

How do I trim an MP3?

Open the MP3, drag the handles to the part you want, choose Keep selection and press Convert, then Download.

Can I cut a part out of the middle?

Yes. Select the part you don’t want and choose Remove selection.

Will cutting reduce the quality?

Choose 320 kbps MP3 for near-original quality, or WAV/FLAC for no quality loss.

Does it work offline?

After the first visit the audio engine is cached, so tools keep working even on a slow connection.
