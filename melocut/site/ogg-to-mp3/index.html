What is an OGG file?

OGG is an open audio format. Most OGG files hold Vorbis music, and voice messages from apps like Telegram and WhatsApp often hold Opus speech. Both sound good, but iPhones, many car stereos and older players won't open them.

MeloCut reads both kinds and saves a standard MP3. If the file is a voice message, the voice note converter page has tips for getting it out of the app first.

Questions and answers

Why won't my iPhone play OGG files?

Apple supports only some OGG files in a few apps. Converting to MP3 or M4A makes them play everywhere.

Does converting OGG to MP3 lose quality?

Both are compressed formats, so a little. At 256 or 320 kbps it is very hard to hear.
