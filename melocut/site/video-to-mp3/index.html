Recorded a concert, a lecture or a funny moment on your phone and only need the sound? MeloCut turns any video into an MP3 you can play anywhere — in your car, in a music app or as a podcast clip.

Supported video formats

MP4, MOV (iPhone videos), WebM, MKV, AVI, 3GP and more. If your browser can open it, MeloCut can almost always convert it.

Which quality should I choose?

320 kbps gives the best MP3 quality and is the right choice for music. 128 kbps makes much smaller files and is fine for speech, lectures and voice recordings. Choose WAV if you plan to edit the audio further.

Questions & answers

How do I convert an MP4 to MP3?

Open the MP4 file in the box above, choose MP3 and a quality, press Convert and then Download. It takes a few seconds.

Can I convert iPhone videos (MOV)?

Yes. Pick the video from your Photos or Files app and it will be converted to MP3.

Is there a file size limit?

Files up to 1 GB are supported. Very large files depend on how much memory your device has.

Can I convert YouTube videos?

MeloCut works with files on your device. It does not download videos from YouTube or other websites.
