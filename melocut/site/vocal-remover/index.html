How it works

In most studio songs the lead vocal is mixed in the centre, while instruments are spread left and right. The vocal remover cancels what is in the centre, leaving the instruments. It works instantly and on any device.

Limits

Results depend on the mix: bass and drums in the centre get quieter too, and reverb on the voice may remain. Live recordings and mono files don't work. Add a little Bass in Effects to bring the low end back.

Questions & answers

Does it work on every song?

It works best on studio songs with a centred lead vocal. Mono recordings cannot be separated this way.

Why is the bass quieter?

Bass is often mixed in the centre too. Raise the Bass slider in Effects to restore it.
