Questions and answers

How do I convert WAV to MP3?

Open the WAV, choose MP3 and a quality, then press Convert and Download.

How do I convert a WhatsApp voice note to MP3?

Save the voice note (.opus) to your device, open it here, choose MP3 and press Convert.

Which audio format is best?

MP3 plays everywhere. M4A sounds a little better at the same size. FLAC and WAV keep full quality but are bigger.

Can I make a slowed + reverb version?

Yes. Tap the Slowed + Reverb preset in Effects, or use the Slowed + Reverb tool.
