Questions & answers

How do I convert WAV to MP3?

Open the WAV file, choose MP3 and a quality, then press Convert and Download.

How do I convert a WhatsApp voice note to MP3?

Save the voice note (.opus) to your device, open it here, choose MP3 and press Convert.

What is the best audio format?

MP3 works everywhere. M4A is slightly better quality at the same size. FLAC and WAV are lossless but larger.

Can I make a slowed + reverb version?

You can make slowed or sped-up versions with the Speed option. Reverb is coming soon.
