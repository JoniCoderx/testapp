FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no sign-up and no app to install.

Can I convert a whole playlist?

No — one video per link. If you paste a playlist link, only the video in the link is converted.

Is there a length limit?

Very long videos (over 3 hours by default) are refused to keep the server fast for everyone.

Where are my files kept?

Converted files are deleted from the server automatically about 20 minutes after conversion.

Is it legal?

Only download videos you own or have permission to download (for example Creative Commons or your own uploads). Respect copyright and YouTube's Terms of Service.
